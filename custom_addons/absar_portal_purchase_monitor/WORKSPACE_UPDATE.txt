ABSAR PURCHASE PORTAL 18.0.1.6.5 - 2026-10-20

Includes 18.0.1.6.4 single-step approval changes.

UI AND PRODUCT CHANGES
- Compact, single-row product grid. Description is an inline one-line field; prices/quantities align as table cells.
- Enter advances to the next editable column. Shift+Enter goes back. Enter at the final column creates the next product row. Arrow keys navigate dropdown choices; Space opens a focused analytic action.
- Product selection waits for defaults before advancing. Current input/focus is preserved when asynchronous pricing/totals redraw the rows.
- Product dropdown has Create and Edit... and Open / Edit Product. The selected product has an open-form icon. Product directory names also open the form.
- Full product create/edit form includes Expense Account. Clearing it uses the category expense account.
- Expense-account field removed from PO line details. Editing product properties from a PO preserves entered PO prices, quantities, text and taxes.
- Previously saved legacy line-account overrides are preserved unless the product is explicitly selected/updated. Their presence is disclosed under Line Details.
- Product updates use dedicated scoped endpoints, Issuer permission, allowed-company product/account selections, explicit field validation and optimistic version checks. Shared product general details apply across companies; its account/cost properties use the selected company. Other-company tax defaults are preserved.
- Dropdowns fit available viewport space and follow their input when scrolling. Late lookup results do not reopen dismissed dropdowns.
- Search and product dialogs have contained scroll areas and visible header/footer within the viewport.
- Arrival/received/billed information moved to the line details (...) action.
- Existing Purchase groups and approval policy retained. Sales, Accounting and Employee Portal Suite not modified or included.
