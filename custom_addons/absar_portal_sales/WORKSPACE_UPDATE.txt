Independent approval is mandatory for the own-document Issuer/Prepare and all-company User groups. The User role expands visibility, not confirmation authority. Save Draft sends no approval notification; Submit for Approval notifies eligible independent approvers.
The original issuer retains ownership when a working user submits on their behalf; the actual submitter is recorded separately and cannot approve that submission. Changes invalidate approval. No internal user groups are implied. Employee Portal Suite and Accounting posting rules are unchanged.
