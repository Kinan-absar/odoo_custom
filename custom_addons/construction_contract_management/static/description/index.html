Construction Commercial Control in Odoo

Built for both revenue-side client contracts and cost-side subcontracts, with structured progress measurement and payment certification connected to Odoo Accounting.

Client Contracts & Subcontracts

Use one construction workflow for both contract directions while keeping partner, project, commercial and accounting context together.

BOQ Management

Maintain contract BOQ lines and section-style display lines as the commercial baseline for measurements and payment certificates.

Measurements

Create measurement records, load BOQ lines and move them through Draft, Submitted, Checked, Approved or Rejected stages before certification.

Interim Payment Certificates

Prepare IPCs from approved progress, track payment status, submit for review, approve and complete the certificate workflow.

Invoice / Vendor Bill Integration

Create the accounting move from an IPC or link an existing customer invoice/vendor bill when the accounting document was created separately.

Advance Payments

Track contract advances and their accounting treatment as part of the construction contract lifecycle.

Variations

Manage additions, omissions and other variation lines with submission, approval, rejection and reset controls.

Retention Release

Record and release retention with configurable release methods, accounting document linkage and payment-status tracking.

Construction Dashboard

Dedicated dashboard data and views provide a consolidated operational view of construction contract activity.

Professional Reports

Printable reports are included for contracts, measurements, IPCs, variations, advances and retention releases.

Optional Employee Portal Integration

The core app works independently. When Employee Portal Suite is also present, the included auto-install bridge can expose authorized contracts, IPCs, variations and measurements through the employee portal.
