# Payment and Receipt Voucher — Recording Demo

Full Odoo 18 module, version 18.0.2.52.6. Upgrade the existing module; keep its technical folder name unchanged so existing records and integrations remain linked.

## Create the examples

1. Install/upgrade this full module in a development/demo database.
2. Sign in as an Accounting Administrator. Select the company to populate.
3. Open Accounting → Accounting → Vouchers → Recording Demo Setup. Depending on the Accounting menu layout, Vouchers may be under the Accounting section.
4. Create a setup, tick **This is a development/demo database**, save, then click **Create Recording Examples** and accept the on-screen notice.
5. Use the buttons on the setup to open only the generated examples, or use the normal voucher and Cash Planning menus.

Installation/upgrade alone creates no sample transactions. The button creates posted accounting examples in the selected company; those affect reports. Existing records and user permissions are not changed. No demo users are created. Existing role assignments determine who can access the ordinary planning and execution menus.

## Included examples

- 10 payment vouchers: draft/posted/cancelled, cash/cheque/bank transfer, bank fees, fully and partially reconciled bills, supplier credit offset, a PO advance and a two-PO allocation.
- 6 receipt vouchers: draft/posted/cancelled, cash/cheque/bank transfer, full and partial customer invoice reconciliation.
- Three weekly plans: previous-week unfinished obligations, current-week execution and next-week planning. Opening balances are illustrative forecasts, not reconciled bank balances. The completed historical week intentionally has deferred/unreceived items and no posted actuals, showing variance.
- All nine planning transaction types: supplier, expense, payroll, cash, journal transfer, loan, customer collection, revenue and other.
- Planned payment examples: not sent, pending CEO review, approved, adjusted, held, rejected, paid awaiting a voucher, paid with a draft voucher, and posted execution.
- Planned receipts with and without weekly assignment; generated draft and posted receipts; unplanned actuals created by the module's ordinary voucher logic.
- Four confirmed POs, including unbilled orders, an invoiced order and one with a draft vendor bill.
- Posted supplier bills and customer invoices, a draft bill, multiple aging buckets and dedicated demo partners, projects, journals, accounts and product.
- A Payment Review snapshot generated by the existing review engine from live source records. It includes Add, Update, Keep, Remove, Convert to Payable and Review cases in a standard installation. The review is not automatically applied.

Sample CEO decisions and payment confirmations are seeded illustrations, not actual approvals or real banking events. No payment instruction is sent to a bank. Accounts, partners and notes identify the fictional examples. Two-PO allocations remain draft so their posting can be demonstrated.

## Recording route

Payment Review → recommendation reason and aging → Apply Selected → Planned Payments → Add to Plan → Weekly Planner → approval/adjustment/hold → treasury confirmation → draft voucher → posting and bill matching → receipt and invoice matching → printed vouchers.

Use the existing roles for approval/execution recording. New sample decisions do not send approval activities to real users. Standard payment review scans the selected company's live sources, so an already-populated demo database may show additional recommendations.

## Repeat and failure handling

One setup per company. Clicking Create again on a Ready setup reopens it without duplicating fixtures. The full build is inside a savepoint; if any accounting/customization rule fails, all new fixture records from that attempt are rolled back. Correct the configuration and retry the same setup. There is intentionally no deletion/reset button for posted examples.

All posted accounting dates are today's date, reducing closed-period problems; invoice and maturity dates supply the aging examples. The old-week plan contains forecast/deferred items rather than backdated journal entries. Existing company localization, lock dates or additional custom PO approval rules still apply.
